# Content drafts

Keep the selected evidence separate from the user's request. Confirm the intended business or personal project from context; never copy private client material into a personal post or public repository. Draft clear, concise content. Name factual uncertainty and preserve source attribution. Save the draft where requested and verify the resulting file or document. Publishing, scheduling and outbound distribution require approval of the exact content and destination. Do not call a draft posted.
